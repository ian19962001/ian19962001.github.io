Beyond the Cheapest Fleet

Safety-Driven Maritime Fleet Optimization

A Maritime Hackathon 2026 project optimizing bunker transport fleet selection by balancing cost, vessel safety, and emissions.

View Slides Certificate

Project Overview

This project examined how stricter vessel safety requirements affect fleet selection, cost, emissions, and operational risk in maritime bunker transport.

Key Contributions

Optimization Scenario Design

Designed fleet optimization scenarios balancing operational cost, vessel safety, and carbon emissions.

Fleet Configuration Analysis

Compared baseline and high-safety fleet configurations under different operational constraints.

Trade-Off Evaluation

Analysed trade-offs between transportation cost, operational safety, and sustainability objectives.

Route Data Integration

Used AIS-derived maritime route data to improve operational realism in optimization experiments.

Hackathon Presentation

Presented the project solution and findings at Maritime Hackathon 2026.
